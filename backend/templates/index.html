Issue Categories Supported

Our intelligent AI model accurately recognizes and auto-dispatches complaints across core municipal sectors.

Potholes & Road Hazards

Broken asphalt, deep craters, damaged speed bumps, and cave-ins assigned directly to Roads & PWD.

Garbage & Waste Dumps

Overflowing dumpsters, illegal roadside waste dumps, and uncollected solid waste handled by Sanitation squads.

Water Leakages & Bursts

Underground pipe ruptures, contaminated water supplies, and low pressure redressed by Water Board.

Streetlight Failures

Dark sectors, flickering light poles, and exposed wiring dispatched to Electricity Board teams.

Drainage & Sewer Blockages

Clogged stormwater gutters, open manholes, and sewage overflows routed to Drainage department.

Public Infrastructure

Damaged bus shelters, broken park benches, and public property repairs tracked transparently.
